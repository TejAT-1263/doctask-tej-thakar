Claims Review Rulebook

Reject or escalate the claim if any of the following apply:

1. Claimed amount exceeds the insured declared value.
2. Claim intimation is made more than 7 days after the incident without justification.
3. Damage pattern is materially inconsistent with the accident narrative.
4. Vehicle was used for commercial hire despite private-use coverage.
5. Driver licence was expired or invalid on the date of loss.
