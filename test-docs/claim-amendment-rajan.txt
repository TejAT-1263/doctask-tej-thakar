─────────────────────────────────────────
PURPOSE OF THIS AMENDMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────
During post-flood restoration work (20–27 March 2024), the licensed contractor
discovered additional structural damage not visible at the time of the original
claim submission on 15 March 2024. This amendment supplements the original claim
with the newly identified damage items only.

─────────────────────────────────────────
NEWLY IDENTIFIED DAMAGE
─────────────────────────────────────────
1. Foundation wall seepage (north-facing wall, 24 linear feet)
   - Cause: Sustained hydrostatic pressure from 14 March flood
   - Assessment: Damp-proofing membrane failure, internal wall plaster delamination
   - Contractor estimate: ₹45,000
   - Contractor: Shinde Construction Works, licence no. MH-CON-2019-04412

2. First-floor bathroom false ceiling collapse
   - Cause: Water ingress through roof joint, worsened by flood moisture accumulation
   - Assessment: Full false ceiling replacement required (80 sq ft)
   - Contractor estimate: ₹18,500
   - Contractor: Shinde Construction Works (same)

3. External boundary wall crack (east side, 8 linear feet)
   - Cause: Soil subsidence due to waterlogging
   - Assessment: Crack width 12–15mm, structural grouting required
   - Contractor estimate: ₹12,000
