Description of Incident:
On the night of 13–14 March 2024, Pune received approximately 180mm of rainfall in
six hours, classified by IMD as an Extremely Severe Rain event. The storm drains on
Shastri Nagar Road overflowed by approximately 01:45 AM, and floodwater entered the
ground floor of the insured property by 02:30 AM, reaching a depth of roughly 90cm
at its peak.

Damage sustained:
  - Ground floor flooring (Italian marble, 620 sq ft): buckled and cracked
  - Electrical wiring (ground floor): submerged, short-circuit throughout
  - Kitchen modular units (base cabinets): water-logged, swollen, unusable
  - Living room sofa set (3+2): soaked beyond recovery
  - Television (Samsung 65", model QN65S90C): irreparable water damage
  - Refrigerator (LG 506L, model GL-T512APZX): motor failure due to moisture
  - Ground floor interior walls: extensive damp patches, plaster peeling

Steps taken after incident:
  - Water pumped out by municipality on 14 March afternoon
  - Temporary electrical isolation done by licensed electrician (receipt enclosed)
  - Damaged goods photographed and listed (inventory attached as Annexure 1)
  - FIR not required (natural calamity, no third-party involvement)
  - IMD rainfall alert bulletin for 13–14 March 2024, Pune: attached as Annexure 2
